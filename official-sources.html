The Composite uses English, Mathematics, and Reading. Science is optional. When Science is taken, ACT also reports a STEM score based on Mathematics and Science.

Blueprint notes modeled by this project

English: 3 long + 2 short operational passages; 2–3 informational, 1–2 argumentative, exactly 1 narrative; subject genre is a separate constraint; approximately 1,390 operational words.

Mathematics: final enhanced reporting-category ranges, at least 8 modeling items, and displayed questions ordered in increasing difficulty.

Reading: 1 literary + 2 informational operational passages; 2 single + 1 paired-or-VQI unit; approximately 2,150 operational words; no displayed form contains two paired units or two VQI units.

Science: 2 Data Representation + 3 Research Summaries + 1 Conflicting Viewpoints operational sets, current reporting-category/content ranges, engineering/design as a secondary code, and current outside-knowledge range.
