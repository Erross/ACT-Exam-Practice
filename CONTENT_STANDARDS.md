# Content Standards

1. Every question must be original and must not reproduce secure or released ACT question wording.
2. Every item has a stable ID, section, reporting category, four choices, one semantic key, rationale, difficulty tag, and variant-family tag where applicable.
3. Quantitative items must be independently recomputed during release review.
4. Distractors must represent plausible errors rather than joke or obviously malformed answers.
5. Variant-family siblings may not appear in the same attempt.
6. Answer positions must be checked for conspicuous imbalance.
7. Section draws must satisfy the current enhanced-ACT operational blueprint before field-test items are inserted.
8. Field-test items are never identified to the student during an attempt and never count toward the estimated raw score.
9. Retake overlap must be measured before release; banks should be expanded when overlap is excessive.
10. Passage-based English, Reading, and Science items must preserve whole-set integrity.
11. A fresh clean-room reviewer must verify the browser-effective bank after substantive fixes.
12. A section remains draft until the entire release gate passes.
