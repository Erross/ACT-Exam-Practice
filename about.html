About this project

What this practice site does — and does not do.

This is a free, unofficial practice resource for the enhanced ACT. Questions are original practice material, not copied ACT test questions.

Current enhanced ACT model

The application models English (50 questions/35 minutes), Mathematics (45/50), Reading (36/40), and optional Science (40/40). The displayed item counts include embedded field-test items that are not included in the official raw score. English, Math, and Reading form the ACT Composite; Science is reported separately when taken.

Estimated scores

Section estimates use raw-to-scale conversion tables from two official enhanced ACT online practice tests. Because real ACT forms are equated independently, and because this site's questions are unofficial, the result is labeled an estimate and shows the range produced by those official practice-form tables where they differ.

Not yet supported

ACT Writing is not currently supported. English, Reading, Science, and full-test mode remain unavailable until their original banks and release audits are complete.
